## Product principles

1. JYC is the source-of-truth layer for its own published records.
2. Historical brochures are context, not automatic current truth.
3. Every current club, person, event, claim and statistic should be verifiable.
4. Do not use fabricated demo data in production.
5. Keep the design restrained and content-first.
6. Motion must not interfere with keyboard, touch or reduced-motion users.
7. Individual clubs may have distinct identities, but the JYC system remains visually coherent.

## Research alignment

The current JIIT public ecosystem reinforces:
- event-first discovery and archives
- clear team ownership
- projects and achievements as proof
- club-specific community pages
- registration and status states
- campus-specific location context

The next release work should therefore prioritize verified content, participation workflows, media provenance and performance over additional decorative effects.
